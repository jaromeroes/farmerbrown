Architecture update — 2026-05-19

Binding Info Stage
New L4 layer · Rebecca per product

After a Specialist (L3) delivers a quote, an optional Binding Info stage collects the underwriting and operational data needed to firm up pricing and bind the policy. The stage runs as a separate agent so it can also receive cold transfers from Customer Service humans.

v0.1 · planning · Rebecca-GL first · Rebecca-BR next
What's new

A fourth layer for post-quote binding

Today the call center stops at L3: the Specialist delivers a price and the call closes or cross-sells. The new stage adds a high-leverage step right after pricing — exploiting the caller's commitment after answering ~15 questions — to capture the remaining underwriting data and book a payment appointment.

Architectural decision

One Rebecca per product line

Decided · 2026-05-19

Per-product Rebecca, not a shared multi-product agent

Each product line (GL, BR, CA, WC, H&A) will have its own Rebecca with its own product-specific question list. Rebecca-GL ships first; Rebecca-BR is next per John ("not too many for that"). The alternative — one Rebecca with branches for all 5 products — was rejected for three reasons:

  • Cost efficiency. The conversational model reloads its full instruction set on every turn of the call. A focused 22-question Rebecca is significantly lighter — and therefore cheaper per call — than a multi-line agent carrying all five products' branches. The saving compounds over the entire L4 leg.
  • Repo convention. Specialists (Jennifer / Sarah / Nora / Wendy / Rachel) are each a separate agent per product. Rebecca follows the same pattern, so engineers don't have to context-switch between two organizational models.
  • Independent versioning. John will iterate on binding questions per product. Per-product agents mean no regression risk to other lines on every tweak.
