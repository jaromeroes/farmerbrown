Internal — Farmer Brown Insurance · v4.0 target architecture

Inbound Call Flow
& Agent Routing

A tiered routing system that handles inbound calls across three websites. Each site has one toll-free number and one bilingual receptionist that triages every call into Sales, Service, or Spanish at the entry point — then routes to the appropriate path. Sales sends new quotes to product specialists; Service runs a closed-menu triage with an in-call certificate-of-insurance flow; Spanish mirrors both branches in a sub-flow.

01 / unified_call_flow

Every site runs the same internal flow inside a single bilingual receptionist. Farmer Brown (Emma), Contractors Liability (Olivia), and Builders Risk (Grace) each handle Sales, Service, and Spanish in one continuous conversation — no separate phone lines, no separate agents per channel. The diagram below describes the flow from the moment the receptionist picks up the call.

tier.a · receptionist picks up · triage question
"Thank you for calling [site name]. Are you looking for a new quote, do you need help with an existing policy, or would you prefer to continue in Spanish?"
The same receptionist hears the answer and stays on the call — no handoff to another agent. The answer determines which of the three branches below runs next.

tier.c · graceful escalation · applies to all 3 branches · all agents
Confusion / stuck / person request: at any point in any branch, if the receptionist (or a downstream specialist) is confused, gets stuck, or the caller asks for a person, the call is warm-transferred to a licensed agent with the appropriate site-specific number.
Silence timeout (~7 sec): if the caller goes silent for ~7 seconds at any point, the agent proactively asks "Are you still there? Would you like me to connect you with a live agent?" — and transfers immediately if the caller asks for one or stays silent. Applies to every agent in every situation.
The system never insists on automation when a human is the right answer.
03 / coi_flow_detail
step.01 · policyholder
Confirm caller identity using the phone number on file. If different, ask for business name and the phone on file.
step.02 · additional insured
Collect name, street, city, state, ZIP of the party to be listed on the certificate. Slow readback to confirm before continuing.
step.03 · endorsements
Walk through three common options — waiver of subrogation, primary & non-contributory, products & completed operations — one by one.
step.04 · contact
Capture a phone or email for the additional insured when available, so the certificate can be sent directly.
step.05 · turn-around
Standard 24h. Expedited 1h offered as a quid pro quo: a review from the caller in return. If accepted: SMS review link + urgent internal alert.
step.06 · cross-sell
Ask about Home & Auto. If yes, promise an SMS application link — does not hand off to Rachel, closes the call in place.

07 / design_principles
· single entry, many paths
Every caller on every site lands on one number, one bilingual receptionist, and one triage question — sales, service, or Spanish — before the flow branches into the appropriate path. Predictable triage, zero dead ends.
· specialists, not generalists
Each product has its own specialist with its own script, tone, and data requirements. A Builder's Risk caller never feels like they're filling out a Home & Auto form.
· audible handoffs
Receptionists and specialists use distinct voices, so the caller audibly recognizes when they've been connected to the right expert — not just a different prompt.
· graceful escalation
Any caller who is confused, frustrated, or asking for a person is routed to a licensed agent within seconds. A 7-second silence on the caller's side automatically triggers a "would you like a live agent?" prompt. The system never insists on automation when a human is the right answer.
