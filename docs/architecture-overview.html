02 · How the call center is organized

Four tiers, each with one job

Calls move through up to three conversational tiers, with a human-agent overflow path available at every step. Each tier is intentionally narrow so the caller is always speaking to the right person for the right step.

Tier L2
Receptionist
Greets the caller, identifies brand, triages intent (new quote / existing policy / specific person / Spanish), and routes.
Tier L3
Specialist
Owns one coverage line. Qualifies the lead, asks underwriting questions, delivers a price in-call when possible, books an appointment.
Tier L4 · New
Binding info
Runs after the price is delivered. Collects the additional underwriting data needed to firm up pricing and bind the policy. Ends with a payment appointment.
Always on
Live agent
At any moment, the caller can ask for a live agent (or trigger a fallback by being silent). The call is transferred immediately — no menu loops.

07 · Bonds intake (new product line)

Bryan · surety bonds for contractors

A new product line under the UnitedSuretyBonds.com brand. Bryan collects everything needed to issue or quote a surety bond, then our Bonding Specialist calls back with pricing. Unlike most other lines, Bryan does NOT quote in-call — pricing requires manual underwriting review.

License or permit bond

Most common · variable price
  1. Required by state/city/town to register as a contractor (general, roofing, HVAC, etc.).
  2. Pricing: ~$100–250/year for permit bonds, $150–$3,000+ for state license bonds depending on credit.
  3. SSN/ITIN required if bond > $25k or in AZ, CA, FL, MD, NJ, WA.

Bid bond

Free · hard qualification
  1. Required by the project owner when a contractor bids on a contract.
  2. Cost: free (supplied at no cost).
  3. Hard requirements: more than 1 year in business · credit score > 700 · no bankruptcy. No workarounds.

Payment & performance bond

3% of contract · hard qualification
  1. Required by the project owner once a contract is awarded.
  2. Cost: 3% of contract price (e.g. $150k contract → $4,500 bond).
  3. Same hard requirements as bid bond.

09 · Architectural decision

One Rebecca per coverage line

Decided · 2026-05-19

Per-product Binding Info agent, not one shared multi-product agent

Each coverage line that adopts the Binding stage will have its own Rebecca with its own product-specific question list. Rebecca-GL ships first; Rebecca-BR is next per John's note. The alternative — one Rebecca with branches for all five products — was rejected because:

  • Cost efficiency. The conversational model reloads its full instruction set on every turn. A focused 22-question Rebecca is cheaper per call than a multi-line agent carrying all five products' branches — and the saving compounds over the entire L4 leg.
  • Operational clarity. Specialists today (Jennifer, Sarah, Nora, Wendy, Rachel) are each one agent per product. Rebecca follows the same pattern — no new mental model.
  • Independent iteration. John tweaks binding questions per product. Per-product agents mean no regression risk to other lines on every change.
