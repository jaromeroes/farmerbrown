How to read this

Only ask what binding still needs

By the time Rebecca picks up, the caller has already answered the full quote interview with the specialist. Rebecca does not repeat any of it — she only asks the extra detail the carriers need to actually bind the policy, kept deliberately short so a committed caller doesn't drop off.

What's already "on file" is different for each product — Builders Risk and General Liability collect different things at quote (BR is about the project & building; GL is about the contractor's operations). So each section below has its own split: what that product's quote already captured (Rebecca just confirms) vs. what Rebecca adds at binding.

Questions for you, John

A handful of decisions that shape the final Builders Risk list. None block the design — they set the length and a couple of branches.

01
Which carrier(s) do you bind Builders Risk through most?
Sizing the list to your primary carrier(s) is the biggest lever on length — the six forms range from very short (Appalachian) to exhaustive (Tokio Marine).
02
How thorough should the eligibility questions be?
We propose a light 3-question version. One carrier (Tokio) asks 20 decline-or-accept questions — we can add more if a carrier you use requires them. Light touch for v1?
03
The GL add-on — capture and follow up, or just mention it?
Should Rebecca take the broker's name and email and route it for follow-up, or simply tell the caller to email info@buildersrisk.net themselves (your original wording)? And does the price stay "around $500" or a firm number?
04
Separate structures — capture detail now, or hand to the service rep?
If they say yes to separate structures, do we collect the count and value of each during the call, or note it and let the service rep finish at the payment appointment?
05
Callers forwarded in by Customer Service without a prior quote
If CS forwards someone who never went through Jennifer, there's nothing "on file" to confirm. Should Rebecca collect a short core profile first, or only handle callers who already have a quote?
06
Claims window — 2 or 3 years?
The quote already asks about claims in the last 2 years; most Builders Risk carriers ask about 3. Re-confirm the 3-year window, or rely on what we have?
